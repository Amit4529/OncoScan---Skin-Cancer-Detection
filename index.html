Understanding the Risk

What You Should Know About Skin Cancer

01

Melanoma

The deadliest form of skin cancer. Develops in melanocytes and can spread rapidly to other organs. Survival rate drops from 99% (early stage) to 30% (late stage).

High Risk
02

Basal Cell Carcinoma

The most common skin cancer. Typically grows slowly and rarely metastasizes, but can cause significant local tissue damage if untreated.

Moderate Risk
03

Squamous Cell Carcinoma

Second most common skin cancer. Can metastasize to lymph nodes and distant organs. Often found on sun-exposed areas like head, neck, and hands.

High Risk
04

Actinic Keratosis

Pre-cancerous rough patches caused by UV damage. Approximately 10% may progress to squamous cell carcinoma if left untreated.

Pre-cancerous

Architecture

EfficientNet-B4 ImageNet Pretrained 456×456 Input Multi-Modal Fusion Focal Loss (γ=2.0) TTA (8 transforms)

Dual-input architecture fusing EfficientNet-B4 image features (1792-dim) with clinical metadata (9-dim: age, sex, anatomical site) via a Dense fusion head. Trained with 2-phase transfer learning.
